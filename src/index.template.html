FIELD GUIDE / 01

Bend the roll. Build the combo.

Every die belongs to an item you carry. A round includes your main turn and the enemy’s main turn. Both sides can react, and share mana and equipment across the whole round.

  1. Build and equip. Spend 3 attribute points and choose unlocked talents. Equip up to 8 items, including a Focus Crystal and a repeatable offensive weapon or spell. Unused items stay in your backpack.
  2. Declare. Choose one main maneuver plus up to 2 supports. The defender chooses one reaction plus up to 1 support. Costs and charges are spent on commitment, even if the attack fails. Neither side sees rolls before committing.
  3. Roll and manipulate. Both sides roll. The acting side manipulates and locks first, then the defender. One free Recollection is shared across the entire round; extra rerolls cost 1 mana and nudges cost 2. Focus costs 2 mana for +2 to an attribute this exchange and skips two future rounds.
  4. Defend or retaliate. Block reduces physical hits and impacts; Ward reduces magical effects. Dodge meets accuracy; Parry meets melee parry difficulty. Counterstrike provides no defense and happens only if you survive. Catch Breath gives half the rolled recovery after the incoming action.
  5. Plan both turns. Every used item is unavailable for the rest of the round, plus its extra recovery rounds. Shield Block and Shield Bash share one shield. Mana regenerates by 2 at round start, or 3 with Meditation. Channel spends a main action to recover mana.
  6. Separate the numbers. Damage modifiers do not improve accuracy. An Aiming Lens improves accuracy, not damage. Weapon venom applies only after HP damage; a Venom Staff applies poison directly. Echo multiplies the primary effect, not accuracy or attached venom.
  7. Interrupt. Shield Bash must meet Poise after defense to skip the target’s next main action. After that skip, Resolve prevents another skip until a later main turn completes. Parry cancels only the current attack.
  8. Round end and progress. Poison ticks simultaneously, ignores armor and block, then decays. Resistance can reduce it. After victory, allocate a point, take a reward, visit the shop and change your loadout. Consumables refill only when purchased or explicitly replenished.
