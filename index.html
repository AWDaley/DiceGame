FIELD GUIDE / 01

Bend the roll. Build the combo.

Jarbound is a short, complete four-fight prototype. Every die performs one function. Its rolled face determines how powerful that function is.

  1. Prepare. Choose up to five ready dice from your collection. Dice on cooldown must wait.
  2. Roll. All selected dice roll together. Inspect the enemy's telegraphed move.
  3. Manipulate. Spend 1 mana to reroll any die, or 2 mana to nudge a result by +1. Recover 2 mana each turn, up to 5.
  4. Attach. Select a purple modifier die, then select any effect die to attach it. Drag-and-drop works on desktop. Stack multiple modifiers on one target; additions resolve before multipliers.
  5. Order. Shift dice left or right to choose activation order. Put buffs before attacks, or add block to survive the enemy's next move.
  6. Progress. Win fights, take a free reward, visit the shop, and forge better faces. Defeat all four encounters to win.
✧

Modifier example: A 4-damage attack with a +3 rune and a ×2 prism becomes (4 + 3) × 2 = 14 damage.
