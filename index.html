FIELD GUIDE / 01

Bend the roll. Build the combo.

Jarbound is a short, complete four-fight prototype. Every die performs one function. Its raw face plus the associated attribute determines its power. Temporary buffs and penalties affect rolls, never maximum HP or mana.

  1. Build your character. Allocate 3 points across Might, Finesse, Arcana, Vitality, and Spirit (maximum 2 each at creation). Every die shows its scaling attribute. Vitality adds 6 HP per point; Spirit adds 1 maximum mana. At 2 Vitality or Spirit, choose a talent.
  2. Prepare. Choose up to five ready dice. Each die has a mana cost, paid when you roll. Budget mana for your dice and manipulation; cooldowns still apply.
  3. Roll. All selected dice roll together. Inspect the enemy's telegraphed move.
  4. Manipulate. You get one free Recollection (reroll) per turn. Further rerolls cost 1 mana, and nudging +1 costs 2 mana. Regenerate 4 mana per turn (5 with Meditation), up to your character’s maximum.
  5. Attach. Select a purple modifier die, then select any effect die to attach it. Drag-and-drop works on desktop. Stack multiple modifiers on one target; additions resolve before multipliers.
  6. Order and counter. Move dice left or right to choose activation order. Against armor, use Platebreaker before attacks or poison to bypass armor. Roll Disruptor on charged turns and meet the displayed stagger threshold to interrupt the move.
  7. Focus. Spend 2 mana before or after rolling for +2 to one attribute this turn. Focus skips two future turns. Enemy attribute penalties last for your next turn and can be interrupted when indicated.
  8. Progress. Each non-final victory grants 1 attribute point (maximum 4 per attribute). Allocate it, take a free reward, visit the shop, and forge better faces. Defeat all four encounters to win.
✧

Modifier example: A face-4 attack with Might 2, a +3 rune, and a ×2 prism becomes (4 + 2 + 3) × 2 = 18 damage. Each modifier uses its own attribute. Multipliers are bounded from ×1 to ×3. Quick Cut is a free fallback capped at 3 damage including Battle Hymn power.
